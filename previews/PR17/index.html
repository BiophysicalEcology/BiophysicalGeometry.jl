API Reference

🥚 Shapes

Spheres, cylinders, ellipsoids, plates and cones sized from mass and density, with the same functions for every shape.

🧥 Layers

Fat inside the skin and fur or feathers outside it, giving the radii and areas that heat passes through.

🐕 Bodies of many parts

A back and a belly, or a torso, head and legs, joined at named surfaces as a graph of parts.

☀️ Silhouettes

The area that intercepts sunlight from any direction, with parts shading each other, and each part's view of sky and ground.

🛠️ Build an animal

An interactive page to assemble an animal with sliders, watch its areas and silhouette change, and copy the code that builds it.

🐄 Tutorials

From a first sphere to a cow and a human, compared with allometry and with NicheMapR.

📏 Units

Every mass, length, area and volume is a Unitful.jl quantity.

How to install BiophysicalGeometry.jl? ​

BiophysicalGeometry.jl can be installed from the Julia REPL:

julia
julia> using Pkg
julia> Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/BiophysicalEcology/BiophysicalGeometry.jl")

Manual ​

BiophysicalGeometry.jl computes areas, lengths and volumes of organisms for biophysical calculations, from geometry: simple shapes, alone or joined into bodies of many parts. The other way to get them, from empirical scaling equations, is the domain of BiologicalScaling.jl.

It is part of the BiophysicalEcology ecosystem for mechanistic niche modelling, where it provides the bodies whose heat budgets are solved by HeatExchange.jl and whose postures are changed by BiophysicalBehaviour.jl. See the Introduction for the design of the package and its origin in NicheMapR.
